# BOXE Backoffice Full Layer Audit - Step 1

Scope: ultra-complete admin-layer parity audit Mines vs BOXE after Wave 5. This pass audits six mandatory layers, not only the Title Editor sub-tabs.

Verdict vocabulary:

- `uguale`: total parity for container, content, visual and functional behavior.
- `diverso - specifico BOXE`: different and justified by BOXE product docs.
- `diverso - gap`: BOXE misses a Mines/platform capability with no valid product exception.
- `diverso - debt`: both games have a surface, but the implementation is divergent or fragile and should be unified.

Important source note: the requested `BACKOFFICE_AUDIT_STEP1_2026-05-21.md` and `BACKOFFICE_AUDIT_STEP2_VERIFIER_2026-05-21.md` are not present in this worktree. The nearest prior record is `docs/games/boxe/BACKOFFICE_PARITY_APPROACH_2026-05-20.md`.

## Executive Verdict

Surface 10 Backoffice is **red**.

The blocker is not the BOXE Title Editor detail page. The blocker is the layer above it: `/admin/games/<engine>` is rich and engine-management capable for Mines, but BOXE is rendered as a flat "Other engines" list with only `Open detail`. That means BOXE lacks master/variant grouping, create variant, inline display-name editing, filters, per-row Save/Preview/Archive/Restore actions, and lobby publication badges in the engine page.

Advanced skin/theme is different by documented BOXE v1 scope (`SPEC.md:72`, `SPEC.md:293`, `SPEC.md:482`, `SPEC.md:942` defer advanced skin), so it is not a pure unapproved implementation miss. However, Wave 5 should not have translated "theme visual specularity with advanced skin out of scope" into Surface 10 full green. At best that was a documented exception requiring product revalidation after Michele's 2026-05-22 observation.
